1st Prize, Web Development: TruthLens AI at HackathonZ 2026 (Hack2Hire)

Malla Reddy University, Hyderabad July 17–18, 2026 Team Kernel Knights (5 Members) 24-Hour National Hackathon

The Challenge & Event Overview

HackathonZ 2026 (Hack2Hire) was a premier national-level 24-hour hackathon hosted by the Department of Data Science at Malla Reddy University, Hyderabad, on July 17–18, 2026. Bringing together over 100+ competing engineering teams from universities across India, the event challenged developers to architect end-to-end software solutions addressing pressing real-world data and media challenges.

What We Built: TruthLens AI

In today's digital media ecosystem, breaking news events are reported with varying degrees of framing, omissions, and political bias. Our team, Kernel Knights (5 members), engineered TruthLens AI — an automated media transparency and narrative verification platform.

Core System Objective: TruthLens AI aggregates news reports from multiple mainstream publications in real-time, extracts the verified underlying factual assertions, and performs side-by-side comparative analysis to identify coverage gaps, sentiment drift, and ideological framing.

Key Technical Architecture & Contributions

As part of the 5-member engineering team, my contributions focused on backend API integration, real-time data ingestion pipelines, and interactive UI component engineering:

• Cross-Publisher Alignment Pipeline: Built automated web scrapers and text normalization modules to align articles from diverse newsrooms covering the exact same event timestamp.
• NLP Fact Extraction Engine: Extracted core entities, statements, and timeline sequences to decouple pure facts from opinion-based editorial framing.
• Side-by-Side Verification Interface: Designed a responsive, accessible web interface presenting side-by-side publisher comparisons, fact-vs-opinion breakdown charts, and objective credibility scoring.

Outcome & Recognition

Following rigorous live evaluation and jury defense against competing university teams nationwide, TruthLens AI clinched 1st Prize in the Web Development category for its end-to-end functionality, high usability, and practical defense against misinformation.
