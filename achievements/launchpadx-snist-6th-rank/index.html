The Competition

LaunchPadX 2026 was a flagship national innovation hackathon hosted by the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Sreenidhi Institute of Science and Technology (SNIST) in collaboration with industry partner VetriaAI. With more than 260 teams and 1,200+ participants competing nationwide, it evaluated breakthrough applications in autonomous systems, AI execution models, and developer infrastructure.

What We Built: Life Admin AI

Most modern AI assistants operate merely as passive text chatbots: you ask a query, and they respond with recommendations, leaving the user to manually switch between apps, copy-paste data, and execute the actual actions.

The Execution Shift: Life Admin AI was engineered to bridge this gap. Leveraging the emerging Model Context Protocol (MCP) standard, Life Admin AI securely connects context across daily tools — email inboxes, calendar schedules, task boards, and communication feeds — allowing the AI to take direct, verified actions rather than static recommendations.

Key Technical Innovations

• Model Context Protocol (MCP) Integration: Standardized multi-app context passing, allowing an autonomous agent to safely read schedules, draft pending emails, and create synchronized calendar items without granting arbitrary API keys.
• Intent Disambiguation & Safeguards: Embedded human-in-the-loop verification checkpoints before destructive actions (sending emails or moving event times).
• Cross-Application State Management: Maintained active context across multiple external service APIs with sub-second response times.

Result & Standing

Out of 260+ competing teams and 1,200+ engineers, team Kernel Knights secured 6th Rank overall (Top 2.3%), earning high commendations from the VetriaAI industry jury for practical architectural design and pioneering application of Model Context Protocol.
