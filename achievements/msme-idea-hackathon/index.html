Program Background

The MSME Idea Hackathon is a flagship national initiative hosted by the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (Govt. of India) to identify and foster student-led technological innovations, enterprise solutions, and startup concepts across India. The scheme provides prototype grants and incubation support to top-tier technical proposals.

Our Proposal & Technical Vision

Our team conceptualized and submitted a scalable software application aimed at resolving operational efficiency bottlenecks through automated workflow intelligence and real-time data orchestration.

Selection Milestone: Out of thousands of submissions submitted by universities and startup incubators nationwide, our proposal was successfully selected for the Stage 2 National Shortlist for jury evaluation and incubation pitch review.

Key Takeaways & Engineering Experience

• National-Level Shortlisting: Validated feasibility, scalability, and market impact against a competitive national applicant pool.
• Enterprise Pitch Preparation: Formulated comprehensive technical architectures, feasibility metrics, data security models, and deployment roadmaps required for institutional incubation grant evaluation.
